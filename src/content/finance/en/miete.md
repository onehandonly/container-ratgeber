## When renting is the right choice

Renting pays off whenever the container is needed for a foreseeable period: for the duration of a construction site, during a refurbishment, for a season or an event. The owner carries the risk of depreciation, often handles maintenance and collects the container at the end.

For simple storage containers the calculation usually tips in favour of buying after about one to one and a half years, for office and sanitary containers later. The calculation is in [Understanding hire prices](/en/ratgeber/container-mietpreise-kalkulation).

## Forms of rental at a glance

| Form | Term | Typical use | Particularity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short-term hire | days to a few weeks | events, moving house, short jobs | high price per day, transport dominates |
| Project hire | a few months | construction site, refurbishment | watch the minimum hire period |
| Long-term hire | a year or more | outdoor store, interim office | discounts of 20% or more possible |
| Rental with purchase option | open | need still unclear | part of the rent credited on purchase, see [hire purchase](/en/kaufen-mieten/kauf/mietkauf) |

## What rent costs and includes

| Container type | Monthly rent (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Storage container 20 ft | €50–150 |
| Office container 20 ft | €80–250 |
| Sanitary container | €150–500 plus service |

Usually not included: delivery and collection, crane work, deposit, consumption and cleaning on return. With short hire periods, transport often makes up the largest share of total cost – see [Transport and delivery costs](/en/ratgeber/container-transport-lieferkosten).

## Simple for tax

For businesses, renting is the simplest route for tax: rent is a business expense, the VAT on it is deductible as input tax, and there is nothing to depreciate or capitalise.

## What to check in the rental contract

1. **Minimum hire period and billing unit:** days, weeks or started months?
2. **Deposit:** amount, repayment and what may be withheld.
3. **Handover record with photos** – on delivery and on return.
4. **Maintenance and damage:** who repairs what, and what counts as normal wear?
5. **Insurance:** is the container insured, and what must you cover yourself?
6. **Termination:** notice period and form, especially for open-ended rental.

The legal details are in [Rental contract and law](/en/ratgeber/container-mietvertrag-recht), the process from enquiry to return in [Hiring a container: the process](/en/ratgeber/container-mieten-ablauf).
